# Business Overview

## Product
AI-guided learning platform that helps users make sense of their performance on tests by analyzing scores, error patterns, and tagged question metadata — then surfacing personalized guidance for what to study next.

## Target Users
- **Learners** — students or self-learners taking practice tests who want to understand *why* they're getting questions wrong, not just their score.
- **Admins / Content Operators** — internal team managing question banks, tags, and platform health via the admin dashboard.

## Core Value Proposition
Most test prep tools give a score and a list of wrong answers. This platform goes further:
1. **Score interpretation** — contextualized analysis of how a learner performed (strengths, weaknesses, trends over time).
2. **Error log analysis** — AI inspects patterns in mistakes (concept gaps, careless errors, time pressure, recurring topics).
3. **Tagged questions** — every question is tagged by topic, sub-topic, difficulty, and skill, enabling fine-grained diagnostics.
4. **AI guidance** — recommendations on what to revisit, which concepts to drill, and which questions to retry.

## Key Features
- Test taking and submission flow
- Per-attempt score breakdown
- Error log with AI-generated explanations
- Tag-based weakness map
- Personalized study recommendations
- Admin dashboard for question/tag/user management

## Differentiators
- Tag taxonomy is first-class — drives both diagnostics and AI recommendations.
- AI feedback is grounded in the learner's actual error history, not generic advice.
- Admin tooling is built in from day one (not bolted on later).

## Status
Early stage. Repo bootstrapped from a starter template; product architecture and feature scope being defined.
